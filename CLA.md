# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Thank you for contributing to KageOps Core. To keep the project sustainable —
specifically, to preserve our ability to offer KageOps under a commercial
license alongside the AGPL-3.0 open core — we ask every contributor to agree to
this CLA before we can merge your contribution.

**You keep the copyright to your contribution.** This agreement grants KageOps
the rights it needs to distribute and relicense your contribution as part of the
project; it does not take your work away from you.

> **Process:** signing is a one-time, automated step. On your first pull request,
> a CLA bot will comment with a link; signing in takes a few seconds and applies
> to all your future contributions. Corporate contributors: see
> [Corporate CLA](#corporate-cla).

## Corporate CLA

If you are contributing on behalf of a company, an authorized signer should
agree to the Corporate CLA (same terms as above, extended to cover employees who
contribute) and list the covered contributors. To arrange this, open a
**private vulnerability report** (Security → Report a vulnerability) or start a
GitHub Discussion on this repository, and a maintainer will follow up.
